      Your little bee guide

      1. Listen to the word. Say the word back to yourself.
      2. Ask to hear it again, ask for a definition, sentence, or part of speech.
      3. Spell each letter aloud with the microphone, or type your spelling. Check recognized letters before submitting.
      4. In practice, try again after a mistake. Words appear when correct. Skipped words appear in your final review.
      5. In a mini contest, submit once per word. All scores and correct spellings stay hidden until the end.

      Practice moves up a level after at least 80% correct on the first try. A mini contest is a friendly, fixed-length practice round, not an official elimination bee.

      Based on Scripps spelling-bee guidance. Local competition rules may differ.

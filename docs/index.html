    Your little bee guide

    In spelling practice, listen to the hidden word, ask for a clue, then type or speak each letter. Check recognized letters before submitting. Choose the Grade 1 or Grade 2 starter pack and one of four difficulty levels. Practice allows retries and says “You got it!” when you spell correctly; mini contests reveal results at the end.

    In Learn patterns, choose one of the sheet’s 11 patterns. The Grade 1 starter pack uses these lesson words; Grade 2 has its own spelling starter pack under Spelling practice. Hear examples, try a short activity, or spell five words from that pattern. Learning examples are visible; spelling answers stay hidden until correct or reviewed at the end.

    Microphone support varies by browser and may send audio to your browser’s speech provider. A grown-up should help enable it. This app does not record audio or save scores.

    Spelling request flow follows Scripps guidance. Pattern lessons are adapted from the supplied practice sheet, not an official app or complete competition list.
